The toggle marked “Piped / CI”

  
It is not a preview mode. It is the invariant, made visible.

  

    Flip it and the colour drains but not one character moves. That is deliberate: the toggle changes CSS custom
    properties only — no text node is rewritten, nothing re-renders. It is the same mechanism the product must use.
    A verdict that reads differently in CI than on your desk is not evidence.
  

  

    Measured on this repository today: the same gate evaluate run into a pipe and under a real
    pty produced captures hashing to the identical
    3b12542229cf67a5…, with zero escape bytes in either. The rule holds now because nothing is
    styled yet — the redesign is exactly what puts it at risk.
  


  
Why two failures are not the same failure

  
Seven distinct causes reach the screen as “this claim is not satisfied”. Only one of them is work never done.

  

    The kernel already separates them with care, and says why: reporting a forgery under the phrasing reserved for
    honest absence lets an attacker choose the wording of the report by choosing which field to tamper with.
    On screen A, tests-executed failed because two records were refused — that is an
    attack surface. diff-reviewed failed because nothing was ever produced. Same verdict,
    opposite response.
  

  

    They are unordered. absent is not a worse failed. Any design that
    ranks them by severity and colours by rank destroys the distinction — which is why the redesign needs a structured
    cause on Evaluation rather than a renderer parsing prose.
